How it works

Three steps, then the file is gone

  1. 01

    Upload

    Drop in a PDF or a scan. It's processed once, for this job only, and it never joins a training set.

  2. 02

    Review

    Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct and deterministic detectors propose every redaction box. You approve, adjust, or add boxes before anything is final.

  3. 03

    Download

    You get a flattened, raster-only PDF. Covered text, hidden layers, and metadata are unrecoverable, and your original is deleted.

The privacy contract

Built like you'd build it for your own clients

Our bare metal, not their API

The model runs on a dedicated GPU worker in the USA. Your documents never reach OpenAI, Google, or another AI vendor.

Processed once, then gone

Files are deleted the moment you download the result, and no later than one hour after upload in any case.

Logs that can't leak

Application logs hold job IDs and counters. Never filenames, document text, or detected PII.

No accounts to breach

Each job lives behind its own unguessable link. There is no user directory and no shared document index.

SOC 2 compliant infrastructure

Your documents are processed and stored on SOC 2 compliant infrastructure in the USA. Encrypted from upload to deletion: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest.

The honest part

No automated system catches 100% of PII. Nothing leaves without your review, and every proposed box is shown before download.

FAQ

The questions your engagement letter would ask

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. The model runs on a dedicated GPU worker on SOC 2 compliant infrastructure in the USA, your documents never reach OpenAI, Google, or another AI vendor, and every file is deleted after download or within one hour.

What exactly happens to my file?

It's uploaded over TLS, processed once for your job only, and deleted the moment you download the redacted result, or one hour after upload, whichever comes first. Logs never contain filenames, document text, or detected PII.

Which documents does it handle?

PDFs and scans (PNG, JPEG, TIFF) up to 50 MB: 1040s, W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, and anything else in a client file. The default policy removes the taxpayer's and recipient's identifiers and keeps payer, employer, and amount fields. You can change any box before download.

Can my firm self-host it?

Yes. The pipeline is licensed under AGPL-3.0 with a noncommercial private-use exception and runs with Docker Compose on your own GPU, so client documents never leave your network.

Is it really free?

Yes. The hosted service is free with no signup, credit card, account tiers, or feature tiers. File, page, request, and queue guardrails exist only to prevent abuse.
